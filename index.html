Emergency Red-Flag Symptoms & Immediate Protocol

Recognize signs that require immediate emergency medical care without delay.

🫀

Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)

  • Crushing pressure, fullness, or squeezing pain in center of chest
  • Pain radiating to left shoulder, arm, back, neck, or jaw
  • Cold sweat, lightheadedness, or sudden nausea
Call 911 / 112 immediately. Sit and rest.
🧠

Stroke (BE-FAST Signs)

  • Balance: Sudden loss of balance or coordination
  • Eyes: Sudden loss of vision in one or both eyes
  • Face: Facial drooping, uneven smile
  • Arms: Arm weakness or numbness on one side
  • Speech: Slurred speech, inability to repeat words
  • Time: Call emergency services immediately!
Every minute matters. Note the exact onset time.
🫁

Severe Respiratory Distress

  • Struggling to breathe, gasping, or blueish tint to lips/fingernails
  • Inability to speak in full sentences without pausing for breath
  • High-pitched wheezing or stridor in the throat
Sit upright, use rescue inhaler if prescribed, call 911.
🐝

Anaphylaxis (Severe Allergy)

  • Rapid swelling of lips, tongue, throat, or face
  • Widespread hives with difficulty swallowing or breathing
  • Sudden drop in blood pressure, dizziness, or collapse
Use Epinephrine auto-injector (EpiPen) immediately.
