Baum-Welch Update Equations

E-Step: \(\gamma_t(i) = \frac{\alpha_t(i)\beta_t(i)}{P(O \mid \lambda)}, \quad \xi_t(i,j) = \frac{\alpha_t(i)A_{ij}B_j(o_{t+1})\beta_{t+1}(j)}{P(O \mid \lambda)}\)

M-Step: \(\bar{A}_{ij} = \frac{\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}\xi_t(i,j)}{\sum_{t=1}^{T-1}\gamma_t(i)}, \quad \bar{B}_{jk} = \frac{\sum_{t: o_t=v_k}\gamma_t(j)}{\sum_{t=1}^T \gamma_t(j)}\)
